Field Lab · Scroll shader

The image holds still.
The scroll breaks it.

Drop an image or video anywhere. Every effect lives on a slice of the scroll track — as you move down the page it fractures into planes, mirrors, prints as halftone, cracks like an LCD, and degrades like tape. Open Controls to pick effects, set where they fire, and tie them to the picture's own light and colour.

End of track. Scroll back up to reverse every effect. Press R to randomise the whole chain, Space to hide the controls.

Chain

Effects run top to bottom. Each one has a ramp (where on the track it fades in and out) and a drive — a feature of the picture that makes the effect stronger or weaker pixel by pixel. The small cyan sliders add scroll motion to a single parameter.

Presets

Saved presets live in this browser. Copy the JSON to share a chain with the team or drop it into the repo.
Halftone, Radial mirror, Broken LCD, VHS glitch and Flow distort are re-implementations of ISF shaders by VIDVOX (flow after Andrew Benson and v002), rewritten here for a scroll-driven chain.
